# Findings: UK airport punctuality, Jan 2025 – Dec 2025

_Generated automatically by `run_pipeline.py` from the CAA data. Numbers update when the data changes._

## Headline

- **1,909,792 flights** analysed across all UK reporting airports.
- **73.4% on time** (early or up to 15 minutes late), compared with 67.1% in the same months a year earlier.
- Average delay **15.1 minutes** per flight; **5.8%** of flights were more than an hour late.
- **1.04%** of planned flights were cancelled.

## Through the year

- Worst month: **Jul 2025** (62.4% on time, 22.0 min average delay).
- Best month: **Mar 2025** (83.6% on time).
- Gap between best and worst month: **21.2 percentage points**.

## Airports (10,000+ flights)

- Most punctual: **Aberdeen** (83.1% on time).
- Least punctual: **Manchester** (65.1% on time, 19.8 min average delay).
- The summer peak cost major airports **8.3 points** of on-time performance on average; the hardest hit was **Gatwick** (-15.9 points).

## Airlines (2,000+ flights)

- Most punctual: **Wideroe Flyveselskap A/S** (92.1% on time).
- Least punctual: **Royal Air Maroc** (51.2% on time; 9.3% of its flights over an hour late).
- Most consistent month to month: **JetBlue Airways Corporation** (±3.2 points); least consistent: **Lufthansa City Airlines GmbH** (±17.0 points).

## Routes (500+ flights)

- Longest average delay: **Heathrow ↔ Kigali** (54.9 minutes, 67.8% on time).
- Domestic routes ran 79.4% on time vs 72.1% for international routes.

## Caveats

- The CAA publishes monthly aggregates per airport, route and airline, not individual flights, so the analysis cannot see time of day.
- Delays are measured against the published schedule; the data does not record the cause of a delay (for example air traffic control, weather or airline operations).
- Average delay is the CAA's figure per matched flight, weighted here by flight volume.
